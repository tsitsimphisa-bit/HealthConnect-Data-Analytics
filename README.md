
# HealthConnect Clinic – Week 5 Data Analytics

## Project Overview

This project forms part of the HealthConnect Clinic Experience Lab. The analysis focuses on understanding appointment attendance and no-show patterns and identifying data-driven opportunities to improve patient support.

## Business Question

How can HealthConnect Clinic use data and AI to reduce missed appointments and improve the patient support experience?

## Week 5 Focus

The Week 5 Data Analytics work covers:

- Data preparation and quality assessment
- Exploratory data analysis
- KPI development
- Power BI dashboard development
- Identification of business insights
- Recommendations for improving appointment attendance

## Key Findings

- The overall no-show rate was 48.46%.
- Longer booking lead times were associated with higher no-show rates.
- Previous no-show history was associated with a higher subsequent no-show rate.
- Appointments with reminders showed a modestly lower no-show rate than appointments without reminders.
- Greater distance to the clinic was associated with a higher no-show rate.
- Waiting time showed little difference between attended and no-show appointments in the initial analysis.
